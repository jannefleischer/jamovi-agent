# Licensing

jamovi-agent follows the licensing of jamovi itself: the server-side code is
released under the AGPL3, the jamovi module under the GPL2+ (like jamovi's
analyses in jmv). The two licenses are compatible.

component                  | license
---------------------------|---------------
`src/jamovi_agent/`        | [AGPL3](LICENSE)
`shim/`, `scripts/`, `tests/` | [AGPL3](LICENSE)
`module/jamoviassistant/`  | [GPL2+](module/jamoviassistant/LICENSE)
